## Orange Pixel Dungeon v1.1.0

### New
- Medic Kit artifact
- Cape of Thorns and Lloyd's Beacon can drop again
- Universal tier 3 talents, plus a new tier 1 and 2 talent for every hero and 3 new tier 3 talents for every subclass (mostly from Re-ARranged)
- New cleric spells: Divine Blast, Divine Ray, Holy Bomb, Resurrection, Holy Mantle, Power of Life and Aggression Induction
- Spyglass, what the Cracked Spyglass turns into once it's repaired
- Added a changelog and version number for Orange

### Changes
- Mossy Clump fills floors with grass and plants, and plants grow back when trampled
- Dimensional Sundial changes enemy health and damage between day and night
- Cracked Spyglass repairs itself after 10 hidden items
- Talisman of Foresight warns about hidden items

### Buffs
- Blessed Ankh gives 1 more turn of invulnerabilty and a shield worth 50% of max health
- Sad Ghost reward is always at least +1
- Parchment Scrap makes enchantments and glyphs 5-15% stronger
- Dried Rose ghost deals magic damage and can wear cursed gear
- Earthen Guardian has more armor and takes 20% less magic damage

### Nerfs
- Parchment Scrap makes enchantments and glyphs a little rarer
- Potions, bombs and Wand of Transfusion can't heal the Dried Rose ghost anymore, Transfusion shields it instead
- Earthen Guardian can't dodge
